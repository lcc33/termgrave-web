so i lowk built this app called Terminal Graveyard

(english version) It helps you keep track of your terminal commands and provides a historical perspective on the evolution of command-line interfaces. So basically every command you run on your terminal get's stored there, with the directory you ran the command and also the date and time. And also the amount of time it took for the command to run. I thought that would be a cool feature to have. With this, you can search through your commands, filter them by date and time, and even copy them to your clipboard.
